Required information to create a Play Console developer account This article describes the identity and contact details you must provide when creating a Google Play Console developer account. This information is intended for new and existing developers. Before you begin, ensure you have a Google Account and a payment method to pay the one-time registration fee. When you create your developer profile, you must provide certain information to verify your identity. The required details differ depending on the developer account type you are creating. For more information, see Choose a developer account type . Create a developer account for personal use To create a personal account, you must provide the following information: Developer name (can be different from your legal name) Legal name Legal address Contact email address (for Google to contact you) Contact phone number (for Google to contact you) Developer email address (shown as part of your developer profile on Google Play) Trader status (shown as part of your developer profile on Google Play in countries and regions where displaying trader status is required, such as the European Economic Area and United Kingdom; to learn more, see Trader status declaration ) Your developer name appears on Google Play, and you can change this name at any time. Google takes your legal name and address from the Google payments profile that you link to your developer account during account creation. To publish apps on Google Play, you must verify these identity details. For instructions, see Verify your developer identity information . Google displays your legal name, your country (based on your legal address), and your developer email address on Google Play. If you monetize on Google Play, Google displays your full address. In certain regions, you must provide additional information that may appear on Google Play, such as your phone number or full address. For details, see Requirements for distributing apps in specific countries/regions . You must verify your contact email address, contact phone number, and developer email address using a one-time password. These must remain operational for the duration of your developer account. For more information, see the Contact information best practices section of this article. Create a developer account for an organization To create an organization account, you must provide the following information: Developer name (can be different from your legal name) From your linked Google payments profile: D-U-N-S number (if your developer account is for a government organization or agency, see the Government organizations and agencies section of this article) Important : Applying for a D-U-N-S number through Dun & Bradstreet is a completely free process. Be aware of third-party services that may attempt to charge high administrative fees for this free registration. Organization name Organization address Organization phone number Organization website Contact name Contact email address (for Google to contact you) Contact phone number (for Google to contact you) Developer email address (shown as part of your developer profile on Google Play) Developer phone number (shown as part of your developer profile on Google Play) Trader status (shown as part of your developer profile on Google Play in countries and regions where displaying trader status is required, such as the European Economic Area and United Kingdom; to learn more, see Trader status declaration ) Your developer name appears on Google Play, and you can change it at any time. Google takes your organization name and address from the Google payments profile that you link to your developer account during account creation. To publish apps on Google Play, you must verify these identity details. For instructions, see Verify your developer identity information . To help improve transparency and user safety, Google displays your legal name, D-U-N-S number, legal address, developer email address, and developer phone number on Google Play. You must verify your contact email address, contact phone number, developer email address, and developer phone number using a one-time password. These communication channels must remain operational for the duration of your developer account. For more information, see the Contact information best practices section of this article. What is a D-U-N-S number and how can I get one? Dun & Bradstreet assigns D-U-N-S numbers, which are unique nine-digit identifiers widely used to verify businesses. You can check whether your organization already has a D-U-N-S number or request one for free through Dun & Bradstreet. Because many organizations already possess a D-U-N-S number for business operations, check your status before you apply for a new number. To search for your number, see the official Dun & Bradstreet website . For more details, expand the corresponding sections on this page. Large organizations often have multiple D-U-N-S numbers for different corporate entities. Ensure that the specific number that you select to create your developer account contains the organization details that you'd like to associate with your developer account. If you do not have a D-U-N-S number, apply for one directly from Dun & Bradstreet . This process can take up to 30 days. You can't create an organization developer account without a valid D-U-N-S number. What if I can't get a D-U-N-S number? If you operate in a region that Dun & Bradstreet does not support, contact Google Play support to request an alternative verification method. Google provides alternative onboarding options only to organizations that are unable to obtain a D-U-N-S number. Support automatically rejects requests with incomplete answers. Google does not grant exemptions or extensions for accounts that fail to provide a D-U-N-S number. Prepare all required documentation before you create your account. Tip: If you must onboard without a D-U-N-S number, contact support before you create your Google Play developer account. Government organizations and agencies Known government organizations and agencies can complete their verification without providing a D-U-N-S number. If the system prompts a government organization or agency for a D-U-N-S number during the payments profile creation process, contact support. Use the Help section within your Play Console account or fill out the Google Play contact form if you don't have access to Play Console. Support can update your account settings to bypass the D-U-N-S requirement. Important: Contact support for a bypass only if you cannot provide a D-U-N-S number. Linked Google payments profiles During account creation, you must link your Play Console developer account to a Google payments profile that represents your real-world identity. To keep your developer account in good standing, keep the legal name and address in this profile up to date. To monetize apps on Google Play, you must create a merchant account. To receive payouts, configure a payments profile. You can select the profile you used during account creation or establish a separate profile. For more information, see Verify your developer identity information . Add and verify your payment method Developers who monetize on Google Play using the Google Play billing system must verify their payment method to receive payouts and maintain compliance with Google Play policies. Note: If you participate in the multi-entity early access program, you will see a setting named 'Google Play billing' instead of 'Payments profile' under Manage account > Play apps settings > Monetization settings . If you configured multiple payment accounts under a multi-entity structure, you must verify all active payment methods rather than only the default method. Add a payment method Before you start, note that only the account owner can manage merchant payment accounts. To add a payment method: Sign in to Play Console, click Manage account in the main navigation menu, and then expand Play apps settings . Under 'Monetization settings', select Payments profile . In the 'How you get paid' section, click Add payment method . Note : Most developers maintain a single merchant payment method. If you have configured multi-entity support, you must select the payment account you want to manage before the 'How you get paid' section appears. If the system requests re-authentication, verify your login credentials. Enter the required bank information, including the bank account name, sort code, and account number. Click Save . After you add your payment method, a notification might state that verification is required. Depending on your region, you will have to complete a deposit challenge or upload official documentation to verify your bank account. The account owner receives an email confirmation when a new payment method is added. If the payment method requires verification, the account owner receives an email detailing the validation steps. Verify a payment method Sign in to Play Console, click Manage account in the main navigation menu, and then expand Play apps settings . Under 'Monetization settings', select Payments profile . If verification tasks are outstanding, the 'How you get paid' section displays a "Verifications pending" status message. Note : Most developers maintain a single merchant payment method. If you have configured multi-entity support, you must select the payment account you want to manage before the 'How you get paid' section appears. To start the verification process, click Verify . Depending on your location, complete one of the following verification methods: Deposit challenge: Google sends a small deposit to your bank account. To complete verification, confirm the exact deposit amount within Play Console. Official bank documents: If Google can't issue a deposit challenge, upload official bank documents to verify account ownership. Verification can take up to five days. Complete this process early to avoid payment delays. Tip: For more information about bank validation, see Verify your identity or payment info . Update your payments profile to match your Dun & Bradstreet profile If you manage an organization, the legal name and address in your Google payments profile must exactly match the details in your Dun & Bradstreet profile. This match ensures the ongoing availability of your developer account and apps on Google Play. Important : Applying for a D-U-N-S number through Dun & Bradstreet is a completely free process. Be aware of third-party services that may attempt to charge high administrative fees for this free registration. To view your payments profile information within Play Console, follow these steps: Sign in to Play Console and go to the Account details page ( Manage account > Account details ). Scroll to the "Developer profile" section. To manage your payments profile directly, click View payments profile next to your profile information to open the Google Payments Center. Important: If you earn money on Google Play, you might maintain multiple payments profiles. To identify the exact profile that requires verification against your Dun & Bradstreet records, follow the navigation steps outlined in the previous section. If Google detects a mismatch, you receive an email and a banner alert within Play Console stating "Your organization name or address is no longer verified." You must resolve this discrepancy before the provided deadline to prevent account restriction and the removal of your apps from Google Play. To approve profile updates directly from a Play Console notification, the account owner must follow these steps: On the Play Console alert banner, click View details . In the 'How to fix' section, click Review details . Note: Only the account owner can perform this action. In Google Payments Center, scroll to the field marked "Action required". Click Review updates and verify the information. If the details are accurate, click Confirm to update your profile. Click Got it to return to Google Payments Center. The field status updates to show it is verified. After completing these steps, refresh your Play Console dashboard. The verification banner no longer appears, confirming successful verification of your legal name and address. Alternative solutions for related D-U-N-S issues Review the following alternative scenarios for D-U-N-S profile updates: D-U-N-S status is out of business: If Dun & Bradstreet flags your profile as out of business, contact them directly to reinstate your profile by logging into your account. D-U-N-S number changes: If Dun & Bradstreet issues a new D-U-N-S number to your business profile, contact Google Play Support for account migration assistance. Contact information requirements Every new Play Console developer account requires verified contact details. Google Play collects and verifies two distinct sets of information: Internal contact info: A contact phone number and email address that Google uses to contact you about your developer account. Google does not show these details to users on Google Play. Public developer profile info: Information that Google displays on Google Play based on your account type. To provide unique support channels for individual apps, configure separate support options on the Store settings page within Play Console. The public information displayed on Google Play depends on your account type. Personal accounts require a verified developer email address (and a verified phone number only if you have a trader status). Organization accounts require a verified developer email address and a verified phone number. You must verify all details using a one-time password during the registration flow to ensure data accuracy. Google Play also requires an official organization phone number. Provide the public business phone number found on your website or within registries such as Dun & Bradstreet. This number can differ from your public developer profile phone number. Google uses this organization phone number for verification purposes and does not display it to users. To customize contact details for individual applications, update your settings on the Store settings page in Play Console. Contact information best practices Expand the following sections to review best practices for managing your contact information (which is not shown to users on Google Play) and public developer information. Internal contact information (not shown on Google Play) You must keep your contact details up to date as this is how Google communicates key information about your developer account. Similarly, ensure that your developer contact details are correct so that users can contact you. Google may occasionally check if your account is active by emailing or calling the account owner using the details you provided, so it is important that these details are accurate. Consider using a contact email address that is different from the one that you used when creating your developer account, especially if your developer account has multiple users or is created for an organization or business. Consider setting up shared notifications for this so the right people on your team can easily access these important messages. The contact email address for an organization or business account should not be a generic or a personal email address. Use an email address associated with your organization. Public developer profile (shown on Google Play) If you have an organization account, you must provide a developer email address and developer phone number to be shown as part of your developer profile on Google Play. Make sure to use an official email address associated with your organization. Google may remove your apps from Google Play if your developer email address or phone number is not accessible. The developer email address for an organization should not be a generic or personal email address.
