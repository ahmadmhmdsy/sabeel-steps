## Detail — why Khatwa was rejected
Play "Khatwa" returns 12 titles: "Khatwa | Musa W" (com.mw491.khatwa), "Khatwa | FEDOUI"
(com.khatwa.health), "Khatwa: AI Fitness & Nutrition" (com.medo.khatwa), "Khatwa – Delivery in
Kirkuk", "Khatwa Driver", "Khatwa" (Value And Growth), "khatwa" (299dollarwebsite), plus others.
App Store returns 8, including "Khatwa | خطوه" (Ali Amer, Health & Fitness, Arabic mental-health
app) and "Khatwa: AI Fitness & Nutrition" (EN+AR).
**Direct concept collision:** a public project named خطوة (Khatwa) already does our exact idea —
Arabic+English, offline ("يعمل بلا إنترنت وبلا حساب"), step+distance, gamified daily mission, streak
flame, lock-phone-until-you-walk, APK v0.10.0, 4.4 MB
(https://ahmad-nayfeh.github.io/khatwa/). Same name, same language, same concept. Rejected.

## Detail — why Sabeel was chosen
Play "Sabeel" returns 7 titles (Saabeel, Sabeel Quran Pro, Sabeel Quran, Sabeel-ul Falah,
Sabeel - The Path, Sabeel Shareek, Sabeel Foundation). App Store returns 12 (mosque water delivery,
Quran, prayer times, "Sabeel | خطوه" mental health). **No step or walking tracker uses it on either
store.** Caveats: sabeel.app currently serves a Blood Pressure app; the word (سبيل = way/path, also
water fountain) is common in religious/charity branding, so distinctiveness is weak: it needs a
distinctive wordmark, and the store title must be "Sabeel — Step Counter" (category-clear).
A real registry check (Iraq + WIPO) is still required before publishing.

## Package id
Chosen: **com.sabeel.steps**. Play ids are unique and cannot change after first publication.
Existing com.Sabeel.health / .customer / .customerapp, com.vga.Sabeel, com.medo.Sabeel,
com.ats.Sabeel_staff are different suffixes, so .steps is not blocked — verify availability in
Play Console at first upload. A later rename means a new id and lost install history.

## Recommendation (unchanged, corrected)
1. Primary: **Sabeel** — no step/walk-tracker collision; "path/way" fits the product. Conditions:
   distinctive wordmark, category-clear title, real clearance check before listing.
2. Fallback: **Pacelet** — coined, cleanest on Play; weaker Arabic resonance.
3. Reject: **Khatwa** (same-concept project), **Stridely** (two step trackers), **Darb** (Gulf
   government transport brands), **Rihla** (travel brand + adidas World Cup ball).
