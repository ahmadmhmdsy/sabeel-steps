### 3.1 High/Critical paragraphs

**L-01 (Critical).** Play's App-content article states: "You must link to a privacy policy on your store listing and within your app, even if your app doesn't access personal or sensitive user data or target children" (answer/9859455). The only policy in the repo is an internal draft whose contact block is literally [DEVELOPER LEGAL NAME], [CONTACT EMAIL], [POSTAL ADDRESS] and [DATE OF FIRST PUBLICATION] (privacy-policy.md:89-91), and the hostable page carries the same brackets (docs/privacy-site/index.html:168-170). The app's Privacy screen is an internal summary and there is no URL link anywhere in app/src/main, so a reviewer or user cannot reach the policy at all. This alone fails the User Data policy and makes the Data safety answers unverifiable.

**L-02 (Critical).** The repo explicitly ships no signing material: README.md:64-65 says "Release builds are unsigned by design (no production keystore in this repository)", there is no signingConfig in app/build.gradle.kts, and no keystore/.jks exists in the tree. The docs' own gate says "R1 Release artifact - FINAL: no release APK/AAB, no release merged manifest" (release-signoff.md:33; release-evidence-log.md:31). Play only accepts AABs signed with an upload key, and Play App Signing enrolment happens at the first upload; until then every declaration, test track and review step is theoretical.

**L-03 (Critical).** Every mandatory App-content declaration is still unsubmitted: FGS declaration plus video (G7), Data safety (G11), Health apps declaration (G12), content rating (G13), listing (G14), app access/ads/audience (G15) - release-signoff.md:19,23-27. The health declaration is not optional for this app: "All developers that have an app published on Google Play must complete the Health apps declaration, including apps on closed testing, open testing, or production tracks" (answer/14738291), and "Activity and Fitness" covers apps that monitor and record physical activities and workouts. The FGS article requires per declared type a description, user-impact explanation, use case and "a link to a video demonstrating each foreground service feature" (answer/13392821) - for health and location. A reviewer comparing "no health features" answers with a step counter will not accept the form.

**L-04 (High).** If the developer account is personal and was created after 2023-11-13, production access requires "a closed test ... with a minimum of 12 testers who have been opted in continuously for at least 14 days" (answer/14151465). The repo records "closed testing 12/14 - not started" (release-signoff.md:30) and the council's Step Zero pilot has not run either. This is calendar time that cannot be compressed by engineering; start it the day the first signed AAB exists.

**L-05 (High).** Store assets and the FGS video are not merely unwritten - the attempted captures are unusable: "four files byte-identical black frames" (EV-16, release-evidence-log.md:41) and "fgs-demo.mp4 = 0 bytes" (EV-15, release-evidence-log.md:40). Play needs real phone screenshots plus feature graphic and EN+AR copy, and the FGS declaration needs a video showing the user triggering each feature. A black PNG or empty mp4 is a rejection, not a placeholder.

**L-06 (High).** The name analysis is explicit that it "is a screening, not a trademark clearance - no registry (USPTO/WIPO/Iraq) was queried" (name-check.md:8,55), and it records MEDIUM risk because a registered Sabeel mark serves a blood-pressure app, with 7 Play/12 App Store names (name-check.md:14). At the same time the package com.sabeel.steps is permanent once published (README.md:7) and Play can remove the app on an IP complaint. Getting this wrong after upload costs the app identity and the 12/14 test clock, so clearance must precede the first AAB.

- Will Play accept "No data collected" when the user can export a CSV to a cloud provider? The on-device carve-out (answer/10787469) supports it and the export is user-initiated, but a reviewer may ask; document the rationale.
- Which FGS "use case" will Play accept for route drawing during a walk, when the TYPE_LOCATION examples are background sharing, navigation and geofencing? The health type's step-counter/exercise-tracker example fits, location is less clear.

- Jurisdiction: worldwide listing by default means EU/UK GDPR-style expectations; the policy has no controller/legal-basis/supervisory-authority text. Iraq has no comprehensive data-protection law identified here; local counsel/scholar review is pending, not done.
- Docs/reality drift: EV-06 says "release build never run / release merged manifest absent", yet a release manifest-merger report appeared around 07:24 during this review and then disappeared after a concurrent build; the workspace was being rebuilt live (debug APK mtime 07:36).
